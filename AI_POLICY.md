# AI policy

Gantry is built with substantial help from AI coding tools, so AI-assisted contributions are welcome. The rules below
keep review time for the maintainer reasonable.

## For pull requests

- You must understand every line you submit, have run it, and be able to explain it. If you cannot, do not submit it.
- Say in the pull request if AI wrote a substantial part of it. A checkbox in the template covers this. Saying so is
  never held against you.
- Unreviewed AI output is closed without comment. That includes large drive-by changes, mass refactors and generated
  tests that do not test anything.
- You are the author of what you submit, and the DCO sign-off is your statement that you have the right to submit it.
  Do not submit code you cannot license under `MIT OR Apache-2.0`, whoever or whatever produced it.

## For issues and discussions

- Write the report yourself, or at least check it. AI-written bug reports without a reproduction on a real Gantry
  install are closed.
- Include what you actually saw: the version, the OS, and the steps.

## The maintainer's call

The maintainer decides what is merged, and can close anything that costs more review time than it gives back.
